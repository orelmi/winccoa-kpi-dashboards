Microstop Filtering

Stops shorter than the threshold are classified as microstops. Microstops are counted separately and excluded from unplanned downtime in OEE availability calculation.

Set to 0 to disable. Typical: 30-300s.
KPI Limits / Thresholds

Define warning and alarm thresholds for KPI values. Warning triggers at the first threshold, alarm at the second. Leave empty for no threshold.
